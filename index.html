Army Pigment is an independent hobby app for miniature painters — a personal paint locker and catalogue for the pots on your shelf, the colours you still want, and the mixes you invent.

Built for iPhone and iPad. Not a store. Not a manufacturer app. Just a beautiful place to keep your collection straight.

Army Pigment is an independent hobby app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Games Workshop, Citadel, Vallejo, The Army Painter, Scale75, P3, AK Interactive, or any other paint manufacturer. Brand names appear only to help you identify products in your personal catalogue. Trademarks belong to their owners.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 App: Army Pigment Developer: Nathan Lynn

This Privacy Policy explains what information Army Pigment handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Army Pigment is an independent hobby app that helps miniature painters catalogue paint pots, wishlists, mixes, and related hobby info. It is not owned, operated, endorsed, or sponsored by any paint manufacturer or game publisher.

Privacy / support contact: armypigment@icloud.com

What we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process the following.

Account information

  • Email address and sign-in credentials when you create or use an account
  • Basic account settings and preferences you choose in Settings

Collection data you add

  • Paints you mark as owned, wishlist, low, or similar status
  • Mixes (including names, notes, and mix photos you choose to save)
  • Other catalogue choices you make in the app

Today, much of your collection data is stored on your device. If we later offer optional cloud sync (for example iCloud) or server-backed backup, that data may also be stored with Apple or our hosting provider under your account. We will update this policy before any such feature goes live and name the services involved.

Device permissions and features

  • Camera — only when you use a feature that needs it (for example barcode capture or mix photos). We do not access the camera in the background. Photos you save as part of a mix are treated as your collection content.
  • App preferences — for example News topics, default paint list, haptics, and similar settings
  • Home Screen widgets — widgets show information from your local collection and/or public hobby news feeds you enable; they do not give us extra personal data beyond what the app already uses
  • Diagnostics — crash or diagnostic information that Apple may provide to developers in anonymous or limited form through App Store Connect

Content we load for News

  • Hobby news headlines, thumbnails, and links loaded from public news or community sources so the News section and news-related widgets can update
  • Opening an article typically opens the publisher’s page (for example in an in-app browser). We do not republish full third-party articles inside Army Pigment

Sharing you initiate

  • If you use share cards or the system share sheet, the content you choose to share is sent to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, other apps). That destination’s privacy practices apply after you share.

What we do not do (current product)

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not use your collection to serve third-party ads
  • We do not collect precise location for advertising or tracking
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across other companies’ apps (if that changes, this policy will be updated before release)

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and run your account and keep you signed in
  • Show your paint locker, wishlist, mixes, and settings
  • Power Home Screen widgets you add
  • Load News headlines and links
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs
  • Respond if you contact support

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate the app, for example:

  • Apple — App Store distribution, device services, Sign in with Apple if offered, and optional iCloud if you turn it on later
  • Future service providers — solely to host accounts, email, or support (we will name them here before they go live)
  • Legal — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service

We do not sell or rent your data to paint brands, game publishers, or marketers.

Third-party brands and news

Paint brand and product names (for example Citadel, Vallejo, The Army Painter, and others) appear so you can identify products you already buy or want. Showing those names does not mean those companies receive your personal data from us, and it does not create an affiliation with them.

News links and images come from public hobby or community sources. Publishers’ sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

Army Pigment is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the email above and we will delete it.

Data retention

  • Local collection data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app
  • Account data is kept while your account is active
  • You may request account deletion via Settings when available, or by emailing the contact above. We will delete or de-identify account data we control within a reasonable time, except where we must retain something for legal reasons

Your choices

  • Use local features without creating an account where the app allows it
  • Deny camera permission (barcode and mix-photo features will not work)
  • Remove Home Screen widgets
  • Delete the app (removes local data on that device)
  • Request account deletion as described above

International users

If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand that information may be processed in the United States (for example via Apple’s services or future hosting). Where required by law, we will provide additional notices or choices.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. We will post the new version at the same public URL and link it from the app (Settings). Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy, except where applicable law requires a different approach.

Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests: armypigment@icloud.com
