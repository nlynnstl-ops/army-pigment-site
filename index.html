This Privacy Policy explains what information Army Pigment handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Army Pigment is an independent hobby app that helps miniature painters catalogue paint pots, wishlists, mixes, and related hobby info. It is not owned, operated, endorsed, or sponsored by any paint manufacturer or game publisher.

Privacy / support contact: armypigment@icloud.com

What we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process the following.

Account information

  • Email address and sign-in credentials when you create or use an account
  • Sign in with Apple — when you choose that option, Apple provides us with an identifier and, depending on your Apple choices, a name and/or email (which may be a private relay address)
  • Basic account settings and preferences you choose in Settings

Purchases and Premium

  • Premium subscription status and related purchase records processed by Apple through the App Store / StoreKit (for example whether you are subscribed, and restore-purchase results)
  • Premium is offered as a yearly subscription at USD $12.99/year (price may vary by region and is shown in the App Store / paywall at purchase time). Premium unlocks may include unlimited paints and mixes, optional iCloud sync when that feature is available and enabled, custom icons, and collection Home Screen widgets
  • Hobby and Creators Home Screen widgets remain available without Premium (free)
  • We do not receive or store your full payment card details; Apple handles billing

Collection data you add

  • Paints you mark as owned, wishlist, low, or similar status
  • Mixes (including names, notes, and mix photos you choose to save)
  • Other catalogue choices you make in the app

Most of your collection data is stored on your device. Premium may include optional iCloud sync of your collection across your Apple devices when that feature is available and enabled in your version of the app; otherwise data stays on device. When iCloud sync is available and you enable it, that data is stored with Apple under your Apple ID according to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. We will keep this policy aligned with what the shipping app actually offers.

Device permissions and features

  • Camera — only when you use a feature that needs it (for example barcode capture or mix photos). We do not access the camera in the background. Photos you save as part of a mix are treated as your collection content.
  • App preferences — for example News topics, default paint list, haptics, and similar settings
  • Home Screen widgets — Collection widgets are a Premium feature. Hobby and Creators widgets stay free. Widgets show information from your local collection and/or public hobby news or creator feeds you enable; they do not give us extra personal data beyond what the app already uses
  • Diagnostics — crash or diagnostic information that Apple may provide to developers in anonymous or limited form through App Store Connect

Content we load for News

  • Hobby news headlines, thumbnails, and links loaded from public news or community sources so the News section and news-related widgets can update
  • Opening an article typically opens the publisher’s page (for example in an in-app browser). We do not republish full third-party articles inside Army Pigment

Sharing you initiate

  • If you use share cards or the system share sheet, the content you choose to share is sent to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, other apps). That destination’s privacy practices apply after you share.

What we do not do (current product)

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not use your collection to serve third-party ads
  • We do not collect precise location for advertising or tracking
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across other companies’ apps (if that changes, this policy will be updated before release)
  • Tracking = No for App Store privacy labels unless we add advertising SDKs (we do not intend to)

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and run your account (including Sign in with Apple) and keep you signed in
  • Provide Premium features you purchase (including unlimited paints and mixes, custom icons, and collection widgets), restore purchases, and reflect subscription status
  • Show your paint locker, wishlist, mixes, and settings
  • Power Home Screen widgets you add (collection widgets require Premium; Hobby and Creators widgets are free)
  • Optionally sync collection data via iCloud when that Premium feature is available and enabled
  • Load News headlines and links
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs
  • Respond if you contact support
  • Honor account deletion requests

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate the app, for example:

  • Apple — App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and subscriptions, device services, and optional iCloud sync when available and enabled by you
  • Service providers — solely to host accounts, email, or support if we use them (we will name them here before they go live if not already covered by Apple)
  • Legal — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service

We do not sell or rent your data to paint brands, game publishers, or marketers.

Third-party brands and news

Paint brand and product names (for example Citadel, Vallejo, The Army Painter, Scale75, P3, AK Interactive, and others) appear so you can identify products you already buy or want. Showing those names does not mean those companies receive your personal data from us, and it does not create an affiliation with them.

News links and images come from public hobby or community sources. Publishers’ sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

Army Pigment is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the email above and we will delete it.

Data retention

  • Local collection data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app
  • Account data is kept while your account is active
  • Purchase and subscription records are also governed by Apple’s systems
  • If you use optional iCloud sync when available, Apple retains that synced data under your Apple ID until you remove it or disable sync according to Apple’s controls
  • Delete account: You may delete your account in Settings, or by emailing armypigment@icloud.com. We will delete or de-identify account data we control within a reasonable time, except where we must retain something for legal reasons. Deleting the app from a device does not by itself cancel an App Store subscription — manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings

Your choices

  • Use local features without creating an account where the app allows it
  • Choose Sign in with Apple (including Hide My Email where Apple offers it)
  • Deny camera permission (barcode and mix-photo features will not work)
  • Purchase, restore, or decline Premium; manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple’s subscription settings
  • Enable or disable optional iCloud sync when that feature is available in your version of the app
  • Remove Home Screen widgets
  • Delete the app (removes local data on that device)
  • Delete your account via Settings or by emailing armypigment@icloud.com

International users

If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand that information may be processed in the United States (for example via Apple’s services). Where required by law, we will provide additional notices or choices.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. We will post the new version at the same public URL and link it from the app (Settings). Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy, except where applicable law requires a different approach.

Contact
